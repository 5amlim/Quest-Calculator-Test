CEDARS | CONTAINER SUPPLY GUIDE

Urine and stool · Collect in sterile cup first, then transfer to the required kit when indicated

Sterile cup: urine

Urine

TESTS Initial collection for urine culture, UA, CT/NG PCR, Legionella antigen and urine AFB.

COLLECT / SUBMIT Separate clean-catch and first-stream collections. Transfer into the required test tube, unless a sterile cup is specifically accepted.

Gray-top urine culture tube

Urine

TESTS Urine culture. Boric-acid urine is also listed as an acceptable specimen for the Legionella urinary antigen assay.

COLLECT / SUBMIT Transfer from the collection cup. Fill the preservative tube to its designated fill line.

Red/yellow swirl-top UA tube

Urine

TESTS Urinalysis (UA).

COLLECT / SUBMIT Transfer urine from the sterile cup into the UA preservative tube. Confirm fill volume.

cobas yellow-top urine PCR

Urine

TESTS Chlamydia / gonorrhea PCR (urine).

COLLECT / SUBMIT Collect first-stream urine in a sterile cup; use the supplied transfer pipette and fill marks.

Sterile cup: stool

Stool

TESTS Initial collection for supported stool PCR, C. difficile, guaiac occult blood, calprotectin and stool WBC.

COLLECT / SUBMIT Keep raw stool separate and transfer to indicated kit vials. Do not use preserved stool for a clean-vial-only test.

ParaPak three-vial stool set

Stool

TESTS Enteric bacteria, parasite and virus PCR; C. difficile; guaiac occult blood; calprotectin; stool WBC.

COLLECT / SUBMIT Standard: send yellow C&S, pink 10% formalin and white clean vials. Respect preservative and fill instructions.

White-cap clean stool vial

Stool

TESTS Guaiac occult blood, calprotectin, and stool WBC / fecal leukocytes.

COLLECT / SUBMIT This single clean, unpreserved vial is also acceptable for the listed tests. It is a vial within the ParaPak kit.

Sterile cup: body fluid / CSF

Other

TESTS Body-fluid culture with Gram stain; nonblood AFB/mycobacterial culture; routine CSF culture.

COLLECT / SUBMIT Body-fluid culture is refrigerated. CSF must be delivered immediately. Confirm specimen-specific AFB volume.

Use alongside current Cedars Lexicomp instructions. Confirm test, collection method, fill and stability.O&P, FIT and Pap are not on the approved menu.

CEDARS | CONTAINER SUPPLY GUIDE

Swabs, cultures and specialty collections · Choose the right specimen and transport system

White-top eSwab

Swab

TESTS Aerobic, anaerobic, throat, wound, abscess, genital and fungal/yeast swab cultures; MRSA/VRE screens.

COLLECT / SUBMIT Use for swab cultures, not as a substitute for PCR transport media. Verify collection site.

Red-top UTM

Swab

TESTS Atypical pneumonia PCR; accepted respiratory viral assays; HSV/VZV; Ureaplasma culture.

COLLECT / SUBMIT Use the specified nasopharyngeal/site swab and UTM per the current monograph.

cobas yellow-top swab PCR

Swab

TESTS Chlamydia / gonorrhea PCR (swab) at accepted rectal, pharyngeal, vaginal or cervical sites.

COLLECT / SUBMIT Use the cobas dual-swab transport kit, following its site-specific instructions.

Affirm / ATTS

Swab

TESTS Vaginitis/vaginosis (BD Affirm).

COLLECT / SUBMIT Use the Affirm ambient-temperature transport system; confirm the current test and kit.

BACTEC: adult set

Blood

TESTS Routine adult blood cultures; aerobic and anaerobic bottles.

COLLECT / SUBMIT A routine adult set uses the ordered aerobic/anaerobic bottles. Verify fill volume before collection.

BACTEC: pediatric

Blood

TESTS Routine pediatric blood culture.

COLLECT / SUBMIT Use the designated pediatric bottle/set, with age/weight-appropriate volume per protocol.

BACTEC Myco/F Lytic

Blood

TESTS AFB/mycobacterial blood culture, fungal blood culture, certain bone-marrow cultures.

COLLECT / SUBMIT This specialty bottle is different from routine blood cultures. Verify kit and acceptance.

Sterile cup: AFB nonblood

Other

TESTS Mycobacteria culture (AFB, nonblood).

COLLECT / SUBMIT Sterile cup is an accepted collection option. A 50 mL sterile leak-proof tube may also be appropriate; confirm specimen-specific requirements.
